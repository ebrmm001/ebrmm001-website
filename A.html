About Our School

Our school is dedicated to providing quality education while fostering a deep respect for the environment. We believe in creating a sustainable future through education and action.

Our Commitment to the Environment

Al Kifah School is a prominent educational institution located in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, known for its commitment to providing quality education and fostering the development of students in a holistic manner. The school offers a comprehensive learning environment that integrates academic excellence with character development, aiming to prepare students for success in both their educational and professional futures.

  • Recycling programs throughout the school
  • Energy-efficient lighting and solar power systems
  • Tree planting and green space development
  • Educational programs on sustainability
